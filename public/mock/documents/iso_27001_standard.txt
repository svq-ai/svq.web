ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — INFORMATION SECURITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
============================================================
Standard Overview & Implementation Guide
SVQ Regulatory Intelligence | February 2025

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
ISO/IEC 27001 is the world's leading international standard for information security
management systems (ISMS). It provides a framework of policies and procedures that includes
all legal, physical and technical controls involved in an organisation's information risk
management processes.

The 2022 revision (ISO/IEC 27001:2022) supersedes the 2013 edition and introduces updated
controls aligned with modern cyber threats and cloud environments.

2. SCOPE AND PURPOSE
---------------------
ISO 27001 helps organisations:

  • Systematically examine information security risks
  • Design and implement a coherent and comprehensive suite of information security controls
  • Adopt an overarching management process to ensure that controls continue to meet needs
  • Demonstrate compliance to customers, partners, and regulators

3. ISMS REQUIREMENTS (CLAUSES 4–10)
--------------------------------------
Clause 4: Context of the Organisation
  Understand the organisation, its context, interested parties, and define the ISMS scope.

Clause 5: Leadership
  Top management must demonstrate leadership, establish an information security policy,
  and assign roles and responsibilities.

Clause 6: Planning
  Address risks and opportunities, set information security objectives, and plan how to
  achieve them.

Clause 7: Support
  Ensure adequate resources, competence, awareness, communication, and documented information.

Clause 8: Operation
  Plan, implement and control processes to meet requirements and implement risk treatment plans.

Clause 9: Performance Evaluation
  Monitor, measure, analyse, and evaluate the ISMS and perform internal audits and management reviews.

Clause 10: Improvement
  Take corrective actions and continually improve the ISMS.

4. ANNEX A CONTROLS (2022 EDITION)
-------------------------------------
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 includes 93 controls organised into four themes:

  (A) Organisational controls (37 controls)
      Policies, roles, responsibilities, asset management, supplier relationships, etc.

  (B) People controls (8 controls)
      Screening, terms and conditions, awareness training, disciplinary process, etc.

  (C) Physical controls (14 controls)
      Physical security perimeters, clear desk/screen policies, equipment maintenance, etc.

  (D) Technological controls (34 controls)
      Access control, cryptography, secure development, vulnerability management, etc.

5. NEW CONTROLS IN ISO 27001:2022
-----------------------------------
The 2022 revision introduced 11 new controls including:

  • Threat intelligence (5.7)
  • Information security for use of cloud services (5.23)
  • ICT readiness for business continuity (5.30)
  • Physical security monitoring (7.4)
  • Configuration management (8.9)
  • Information deletion (8.10)
  • Data masking (8.11)
  • Data leakage prevention (8.12)
  • Monitoring activities (8.16)
  • Web filtering (8.23)
  • Secure coding (8.28)

6. RISK ASSESSMENT PROCESS
----------------------------
ISO 27001 requires a formal risk assessment to:

  1. Identify information assets and their owners
  2. Identify threats to and vulnerabilities of those assets
  3. Assess the likelihood and impact of risks materialising
  4. Evaluate risk levels against defined risk acceptance criteria
  5. Select appropriate risk treatment options
  6. Produce a Statement of Applicability (SoA)

7. CERTIFICATION PROCESS
--------------------------
Stage 1 (Documentation Review):
  The certification body reviews the ISMS documentation to ensure it meets ISO 27001 requirements.

Stage 2 (Certification Audit):
  An on-site audit assesses whether the ISMS has been implemented and is operating effectively.

Surveillance Audits:
  Annual surveillance audits check that the ISMS is being maintained.

Recertification:
  Full recertification audit every three years.

8. RELATIONSHIP WITH GDPR
---------------------------
ISO 27001 is closely aligned with GDPR requirements. Certification can demonstrate:

  • Implementation of appropriate technical and organisational measures (Article 32 GDPR)
  • Evidence of data protection by design and by default (Article 25 GDPR)
  • Systematic approach to breach detection and response (Articles 33–34 GDPR)
  • Accountability through documented policies and procedures (Article 5(2) GDPR)

9. KEY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
---------------------------
Mandatory documented information includes:

  • ISMS scope document
  • Information security policy
  • Risk assessment and treatment methodology
  • Statement of Applicability (SoA)
  • Risk treatment plan
  • Risk assessment results
  • Competence evidence
  • Results of monitoring and measurement
  • Internal audit programme and results
  • Management review results
  • Nature of nonconformities and corrective actions
